# Import Pipeline Overview

**Data Sources**:
1. **CSV Files** - Primary business data import
   - Bulk business listings
   - Contact information
   - Business categories and descriptions

2. **Google Places API** - Business enhancement
   - Photos and additional details
   - Reviews and ratings
   - Geolocation data
   - Opening hours

3. **Future Sources**:
   - Additional business directories
   - Social media integrations
   - Government business registries

**Import Process**:
1. Data validation and cleaning
2. Duplicate detection and merging
3. Category mapping and standardization
4. Geocoding and location enrichment
5. SEO-friendly URL generation
6. Multi-language content detection

**Target**: Focus on "one person companies / solo businesses" for better market fit.

**Proven Scale**: Previous imports successfully processed 6k+ businesses.
